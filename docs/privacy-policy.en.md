# Halt Privacy Policy

<!-- English version of docs/privacy-policy.md, served at https://halt.aethereal.top/en/privacy. Keep both in sync. -->

Effective from: October 16, 2026

Halt is a browser extension by Aethereal (aethereal.top) that warns you about phishing sites and dangerous wallet transaction requests. This policy explains what data is processed and where it goes.

## In short

- Site checks happen **on your device**. The list of sites you visit is never sent anywhere.
- Warnings and their explanations are also produced **on your device**. The extension sends no data to our servers or to anyone else.
- We **never** collect your wallet address, balance, seed phrase, or private key.

## Data processed

| Data | Leaves your device? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Addresses of sites you visit | **No** | Checked on your device against the lists and model bundled in the extension. |
| Domain of a site that triggers a warning | **No** | The warning is shown directly by the extension. |
| Transaction requests that trigger a warning | **No** | Read and displayed on your device. |
| Wallet address, balance, seed phrase, private key | **Never** | |
| Page content, forms, passwords | **Never** | |

The check result for each tab is kept temporarily in your browser (`chrome.storage.session`) so the popup can show it, and is cleared when the browser closes.

## Where data is sent

Nowhere. The extension has no server and makes no network requests. The phishing list and detection model ship inside the extension and are updated through extension updates on the Chrome Web Store.

The "Report" link only opens our community page in a new tab; anything you write there is subject to that platform's policies.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share data with advertisers.
- We don't build user profiles or accounts.
- We don't load code from outside the extension.

## Limits

Halt is **not a security guarantee**. New sites or scams may not be detected yet. Always double-check the site address and the transaction details in your wallet.

## Contact

Questions, or was your official site flagged by mistake? Contact us at [aethereal.hq@gmail.com](mailto:aethereal.hq@gmail.com).

Changes to this policy will be announced on this page together with their effective date.
