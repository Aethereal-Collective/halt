# Chrome Web Store listing

Copy to paste into the CWS developer dashboard. Listing language: Bahasa Indonesia (primary), English (secondary).

## Single purpose
Halt warns users about crypto phishing websites and risky wallet transaction or signature requests.

## Short description (≤132 chars)
- ID: Lindungi aset kripto kamu dari situs phishing dan transaksi wallet berbahaya. Semua pemeriksaan berjalan di perangkat kamu.
- EN: Protect your crypto from phishing sites and dangerous wallet requests. Every check runs on your device.

## Detailed description (ID)
Halt memeriksa setiap situs yang kamu buka dan setiap permintaan wallet yang berisiko, lalu menjelaskan bahayanya dengan bahasa yang mudah dipahami. Tersedia dalam Bahasa Indonesia dan English.

- **Situs palsu:** memblokir situs tiruan exchange dan dApp populer (misalnya indodaz.com yang meniru indodax.com) sebelum kamu sempat mengetik apa pun.
- **Transaksi berbahaya (EVM):** menahan izin token tanpa batas, izin semua NFT (setApprovalForAll), tanda tangan Permit/Permit2, listing NFT seharga 0, dan eth_sign. Kamu yang memutuskan: Tolak atau lanjut ke wallet.
- **Penjelasan jelas:** setiap peringatan menjelaskan apa yang bisa terjadi pada aset kamu dan apa yang sebaiknya kamu lakukan.
- **Privasi penuh:** semua pemeriksaan berjalan di perangkat kamu. Tidak ada data yang dikirim ke mana pun.

Mendukung MetaMask, Rabby, OKX Wallet, dan wallet EVM lain di Chrome dan Brave.

Bukan jaminan keamanan. Selalu cek ulang alamat situs dan isi transaksi di wallet kamu.

Daftar phishing dari MetaMask eth-phishing-detect dan Polkadot.js phishing (Apache-2.0). Data pelatihan model lokal juga memakai Phishing.Database (MIT) dan Majestic Million (CC BY 3.0).

## Detailed description (EN)
Halt checks every site you open and every risky wallet request, then explains the danger in plain language. Available in English and Bahasa Indonesia.

- **Fake sites:** blocks copies of popular exchanges and dApps (for example indodaz.com pretending to be indodax.com) before you can type anything, and points you to the official site.
- **Dangerous transactions (EVM):** holds unlimited token approvals, approval for all NFTs (setApprovalForAll), Permit/Permit2 signatures, zero-price NFT listings, and eth_sign. You decide: reject, or continue to your wallet.
- **Clear warnings:** every warning says what could happen to your assets and what to do next.
- **Fully private:** every check runs on your device. No data is sent anywhere.

Works with MetaMask, Rabby, OKX Wallet, and other EVM wallets on Chrome and Brave.

Not a security guarantee. Always double-check the site address and the transaction details in your wallet.

Phishing lists from MetaMask eth-phishing-detect and Polkadot.js phishing (Apache-2.0). The on-device model was also trained on Phishing.Database (MIT) and Majestic Million (CC BY 3.0).

## Permission justifications
| Permission | Justification |
|---|---|
| Content script on `<all_urls>` | The content script must run on every site the user opens to check the site's domain locally against the bundled phishing list and model, and to watch for risky wallet requests on any dApp. Nothing is sent off the device. |
| `storage` | Keeps the per-tab check result in session storage so the toolbar popup can show it (cleared when the browser closes), and remembers the user's language choice (Bahasa Indonesia or English) in local storage. Nothing is sent off the device. |
| Remote code | None. All code ships in the package, and the extension makes no network requests. |
